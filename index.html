When will my order arrive?

Delivery is approximately 25 days from the batch ship date—not from the day you submit your request. We’ll confirm the expected batch timing with you. Customs, cargo schedules, and holidays can cause delays.

How do you check authenticity?

We source from official brands and approved retailers. Amazon requests must be sold by Amazon. Other stores are reviewed before purchase. Invoice or purchase proof is available on request. Sports Solutions is an independent sourcing service.

What does my estimate include?

The calculator multiplies the USD product price by the estimate exchange rate, then adds the listed service fee. Store tax, checkout fees, and any other applicable charges will be clarified in your final quote. Equipment requires a custom service quote.

How do payment and confirmation work?

We share payment instructions through WhatsApp after reviewing your request. Confirm the final price, payment schedule, and delivery details with us before making a bKash or bank payment. Submitting this form does not reserve or purchase an item.

What about sizes, cancellations, or problems?

Please check the store’s size chart and confirm the exact size and variant before purchase. Discuss cancellation and refund terms with us before confirming your order; cancellation may not be possible once purchased. If we purchase the wrong item due to our error, we’ll work with you to correct it. Packaging may show minor marks from transport; report any product damage promptly.
